# Episode 06: See what you changed before you save.

[mark hook]
Before you save, Rubrique shows you exactly what you changed.
[pause 600]

[mark edit]
I am tightening the spec before the agents start. I open this paragraph
and add a rule: log every export with the caller ID.
[pause 800]
[mark edit-done]
and a bar appears beside it. The bar means: edited since the last save.
[pause 500]

[mark delete]
Now I delete a block I no longer need. A dashed line marks where it was.
[pause 600]

[mark hover]
Hover the bar to see the saved text next to the new one.
[pause 400]
[mark revert]
If I prefer the old version, Revert would put it back with one click.
[pause 600]

[mark status]
The status bar counts every change.
[mark panel]
Click it, and the Changes panel lists each block that will change when I
save.
[pause 800]

[mark close]
And if I am not ready to save at all? I close the tab and choose Keep for
later.
[pause 600]
[mark reopen]
When I open the file again, my edits come back.
[pause 800]

[mark recap]
So: bars for edits, dashed lines for deletions, and nothing is lost until
you decide.
[pause 800]
